Located in West Cairo, Waslet Dahshour has become one of the vibrant dining, social, and commercial hubs in El Sheikh Zayed. It features many restaurants, cafes, entertainment venues, and shopping malls, serving both Zayed’s and 6 October’s residents. Waslet Dahshour is connected to every corner of the city, as well as the surrounding areas.
